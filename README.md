# Ali Fernandes

I build software, modernize systems, and help organizations put technology to work.

My work spans product development, infrastructure, security, and organizational change. I turn problems into requirements, work through the technical decisions, and carry implementation through to the people who need to use it. As a former CIO, I bring experience owning the roadmap, the rollout, and the operations that follow.

Today, I apply that experience to AI-assisted development and AI enablement: building useful tools, redesigning workflows, and helping teams understand where AI fits and how to use it responsibly.

## How I work

- Start with the problem and the people doing the work.
- Connect product requirements to architecture, data, and access controls.
- Build in increments, verify behavior, and document the decisions.
- Make limitations and human review points explicit.
- Treat training, adoption, and ongoing operations as part of delivery.

## A current example

**[Market Action Dynamics](https://github.com/ali-fernandes/mad-platform-case-study)** brings this approach into a market research and planning platform. Its workspaces combine publishing, research, model evaluation, event timing, and private performance analytics.

The project shows how I approach application architecture, evidence quality, authorization, data reconciliation, and release verification. It is a current expression of the same practice I bring to technology implementation and organizational transformation.
